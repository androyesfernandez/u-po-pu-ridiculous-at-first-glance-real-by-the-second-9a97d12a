One sentence pitching a startup that sounds absurd until the numbers land: mall kiosks selling professionally written apologies, delivered in person by a trained actor to your spouse — priced against what a divorce costs. Written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). The pitch is 190 characters, inside the 1–200 character gate.
